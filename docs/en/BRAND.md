Use the monochrome assets without stretching, recoloring, glow or additional backgrounds. Preserve their built-in clear space. Interface controls retain neutral semantic states and native labels; the symbol is not a substitute for readable action names.

The selected symbol and banner were produced with `image_gen.imagegen` on 2026-10-12. Their exact prompts are embedded in every raster and retained in [provenance](../../playground/public/brand/provenance.json). Size/format derivatives preserve the generated artwork. Rejected transparent trials are not shipped. The banner's rectangular diagram is brand illustration, not query telemetry or measured evidence.
